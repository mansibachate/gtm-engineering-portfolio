# 01 · ICP & Account Research

**Status:** in progress

## Problem

Before a sales team reaches out to anyone, it needs to know who it should be selling to and why each target account is a good fit. A BDR typically spends 1–2 hours researching a single company by hand.

## Approach

1. Pick a real SaaS product and define its ideal customer profile (ICP)
2. Choose target accounts that could plausibly fit
3. Run each account through a 5-part research process:
   - **Company fit:** firmographics, stage, growth signals
   - **Contacts:** the buying committee and decision-makers
   - **Opportunity:** BANT qualification (budget, authority, need, timing)
   - **Competition:** what they likely use today
   - **Strategy:** the angle and the outreach sequence
4. Combine the scores into an overall grade and next action
