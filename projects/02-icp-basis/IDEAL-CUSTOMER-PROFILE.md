# Ideal Customer Profile: Basis

**Product:** [Basis](https://www.getbasis.ai): AI agents that do accounting, tax, and audit work end to end, with a human accountant reviewing the output
**Written as:** a BDR selling Basis
**Type:** hypothesis ICP, built from public sources (no access to Basis's CRM data). Figures are marked *(reported)* when they come from press or the company, and *(est.)* when they are my estimates.
**Date:** 28 Oct 2026

---

## 0. Why this ICP looks nothing like Clay's

| | Clay (project 01) | Basis (this project) |
|---|---|---|
| **Buyer's industry** | B2B tech, sales-led | **Professional services:** accounting and CPA firms |
| **How companies are measured** | Employees, ARR, funding stage | Employees, **firm revenue, national ranking** (Top 100/200/400 lists) |
| **Ownership** | VC-backed startups | **Partnerships**, increasingly **private-equity backed** |
| **What the product replaces** | Manual research hours | **Billable staff hours** in tax, close, and audit |
| **Strongest timing signal** | Hiring a GTM engineer | **Busy-season capacity pain** and **PE investment / mergers** |
| **Who signs** | VP Sales / CRO | **Managing partner** (often with the partner group) |

**Lesson:** the ICP framework stays the same; every value inside it changes with the market.

## 1. Firmographics

| Attribute | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Industry** | US accounting and CPA firms with **CAS** (client accounting services), **tax**, and/or **audit** practices | These are Basis's 3 core agent areas *(reported)* |
| **Size** | **50–3,000 employees** (sweet spot 150–1,500) | Big enough for volume and an innovation budget; small enough to decide without a Big 4 procurement process. Basis reports ~30% of the Top 25 and ~20% of the Top 150 firms as users *(reported)*, so the next tier down is the open ground. |
| **Ranking** | Accounting Today **Top 100** / INSIDE Public Accounting **Top 400** firms, ranked ~25–400 | These public lists are a ready-made TAM |
| **Geography** | United States first | All named customers are US firms *(reported)*; US tax rules (1065, K-1s) are what the tax agent handles |
| **Ownership** | PE-backed, recently merged, or growth-focused partnerships | PE owners push for margin and tech adoption (est.) |
| **Secondary segment** | In-house corporate accounting teams at 200–2,000-employee companies | Basis has a Corporate Accounting product, but the firm segment is where its customer logos are |

## 2. Technographics

| Signal | What it suggests |
|---|---|
| **Client ledgers on QuickBooks Online / Xero** (in the CAS practice) | Direct fit: Basis works on top of these *(reported)* |
| **Tax software:** CCH Axcess, UltraTax CS, GoSystem | Tax practice at scale; check how Basis fits their workflow |
| **Practice management:** Karbon, CCH iFirm, Canopy | The firm already buys modern software |
| **Audit tools:** DataSnipper, CaseWare | An audit practice that already automates; an innovation-friendly culture |
| **A "Director of Innovation" or "Chief Transformation Officer"** | Someone whose job is to buy tools like this |
| **Red flag:** paper-heavy, desktop-only, no tech roles | Too early for agents |

## 3. Buying signals (timing)

Ranked strongest first:

1. **PE investment or a merger announced in the last 12 months.** New owners want more revenue per employee, and integration is a moment to change tools.
2. **Hiring for tax preparers, seasonal staff, or staff accountants with many open roles.** The talent shortage is the core pain; open roles are visible proof.
3. **Launching or expanding a CAS practice** (press release, new CAS leader, new service page). CAS month-end close is a core Basis agent.
4. **A new innovation, transformation, or CIO hire.** That person needs a quick win.
5. **Timing in the year:** reach tax leaders **May–August and October–December**, after busy seasons end (April 15, Oct 15 extensions), while the pain is fresh and they have time to evaluate.
6. **Public talk about AI** by a partner (LinkedIn posts, conference talks at AICPA ENGAGE, Accounting Today events).

## 4. Pain points

1. **Not enough accountants.** Fewer CPA candidates *(widely reported)*; firms turn down work.
2. **Busy-season burnout:** 60–80-hour weeks hurt retention.
3. **Margin pressure on fixed-fee work:** a fixed-fee return that takes 100+ hours loses money. (Boulay reports complex returns falling from 100+ prep hours to 15–20 with Basis *(reported, testimonial)*.)
4. **CAS can't scale:** each new client needs more staff for month-end close.
5. **Offshoring is the current fix**, with quality, security, and time-zone issues.

## 5. Budget

- **Pricing:** not public; demo-led sales *(reported)*. Expect **enterprise contracts** priced per firm, practice, or volume, likely **five to six figures a year** for a mid-size firm *(est.)*.
- **How to justify it:** compare with staff cost. If one agent saves 1,000 preparer hours a season at a $60–100 loaded cost per hour *(est.)*, that's $60k–100k of capacity.
- **Who has budget:** managing partner or firm COO/CFO; innovation budgets at larger firms.

## 6. Personas (buying committee)

| Role | Persona | Cares about | Message angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Economic buyer** | Managing partner / CEO | Margin, growth without hiring, partner buy-in | "Take on more clients this season without more staff" |
| **Champion** | Tax practice leader, CAS leader, or audit leader | Busy-season hours, staff burnout, quality | "Your team reviews instead of prepares" |
| **Technical evaluator** | CIO / Director of Innovation | Security, data handling, integrations | Security page, SOC reports, integration details |
| **Influencer** | Senior managers who'd use it | Will it make my work harder? | Accuracy, citations, review workflow |

**Note:** accounting firms are **partnerships**, so big purchases often need several partners to agree. Expect longer cycles and a pilot (one practice, one season) before a firm-wide rollout.

## 7. Negative ICP (don't prospect)

1. **Big 4** (Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG): build their own AI and run long procurement cycles; a different motion entirely
2. **Firms already on Basis** (check the logo wall: Baker Tilly, CohnReznick, Wiss, Citrin Cooperman, Sikich, UHY, and others *(reported)*). These are expansion accounts for customer success, not outbound.
3. **Solo practitioners and firms under ~10 people:** Basis has a separate small-firm offer; not worth a BDR's time
4. **Bookkeeping-only shops** with no tax or close work
5. **Firms outside the US**, until Basis shows non-US tax capability
6. **Firms that just signed a competing AI platform** (12-month lockout)
7. **Firms mid-busy season** for a tax pitch (Feb–April 15, Sept–Oct 15): no one will take the meeting

## 9. Prospecting playbook

1. **Build the TAM** from the public **Accounting Today Top 100** and **IPA Top 400** lists
2. **Remove** the Big 4 and current Basis customers
3. **Layer signals:** PE/merger news (search "acquires CPA firm" / "private equity accounting firm"), job-board counts for tax preparers, CAS announcements
4. **Find people:** managing partner, tax leader, CAS leader, CIO/innovation leader
5. **Personalize** around the firm's own signal and the busy-season calendar
6. **Send in the right window** (May–Aug, Oct–Dec)

## 10. Competitive context

| Alternative | How Basis compares (positioning) |
|---|---|
| **More hiring / offshore staff** | The real competitor. Basis pitches agents doing the prep with a human reviewing. |
| **Rillet, Digits, Puzzle** | AI-native **ledgers** that replace the accounting system; Basis works **on top of** existing ledgers for firms *(reported)* |
| **DataSnipper, Numeric** | Point tools for audit or close; Basis pitches end-to-end agents across tax, CAS, and audit |
| **Built-in AI** from Intuit, Thomson Reuters, Wolters Kluwer | Already in the tax stack; "good enough" risk |
| **General AI tools** (ChatGPT, Claude) | Cheap, but no workflow, citations, or audit trail |
